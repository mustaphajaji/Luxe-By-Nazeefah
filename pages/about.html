ABOUT LUXE BY NAZEEFAH

OUR STORY

Luxe by Nazeefah is a Gombe based fashion brand founded to make elegant, modest clothing and finishing pieces easy to find in one trusted place. Each piece is chosen with care for its fabric, finish and the way it makes the wearer feel.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Abayas
  • Adire outfits
  • Shoes
  • Bags
  • Jewellery and accessories

OUR VISION

To be a name customers trust for quality and elegance, dressing every woman with confidence and treating every enquiry with the attention it deserves, so that each customer leaves satisfied.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Nazeefah

Founder, Luxe by Nazeefah

Nazeefah started the brand from a love of graceful, modest dressing and a belief that quality should feel personal. She selects every piece herself and speaks with each customer directly, from first enquiry to final fitting advice.
